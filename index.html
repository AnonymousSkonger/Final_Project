System Overview & Problem Statement

The Problem: Clinics and hospitals often experience severe friction in matching patients to the correct appointed doctor, leading to dangerous bottlenecks—especially in life-threatening situations.

The Proposed Solution: Developed using Django, our platform gives receptionists an efficient command center to record patient ailments, match specialties (e.g., Cardiology for chest pains, Orthopedics for trauma), and flag urgent emergencies immediately.

Who it's for: Receptionists manage scheduling and queue prioritization; Doctors verify expected patients and update consultation statuses; Patients gain transparency over arrival times and doctor assignments.
